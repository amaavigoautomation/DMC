B2B Distribution for DMCs: What Agents Actually Need

Agent onboarding, pricing, credit, inventory and booking workflows.

A travel agent uses your B2B portal to answer a customer’s question and move a sale forward. The experience needs to make those tasks easier while reflecting the commercial relationship between your businesses.

Product choice matters, but so do rate clarity, booking status, payment arrangements and access to support.

An effective B2B platform brings those requirements together without asking the agent to repeatedly contact your office for routine information.

A clear route from registration to access

Agent onboarding establishes who is applying, which company they represent and what information your team needs before approval.

Registration, document review and commercial setup should form a defined process. Access should follow the appropriate approval and configuration rather than leave the agent uncertain about whether the account is ready.

DMC24 supports agent self-registration, KYC review and approval workflows as part of its B2B approach.

Products with enough detail to sell

An agent needs to understand what a product includes, which dates or conditions apply and whether it suits the traveller’s requirements.

A service name and price may not be sufficient. Pickup arrangements, room details, inclusions and applicable cancellation conditions can influence the purchasing decision.

Presenting relevant information at the point of selection helps the agent explain the product accurately to the customer.

Commercial rules that match the relationship

The portal should reflect the agreed pricing and payment arrangement for the agent. Market-specific or agent-specific markups, approved credit and supported currencies need to be considered during setup.

Credit access also needs a clear operating process. Agents and the DMC team should understand the applicable arrangement and how to handle transactions that require a payment or further review.

The objective is to make routine business predictable while keeping commercial control with the DMC.

Honest booking status

Some products can follow an instant booking workflow. Others require a request and supplier confirmation.

The distinction must remain clear to the agent. A submitted request should not be presented as a confirmed service before the required confirmation has been obtained.

Reliable status information lets the agent communicate accurately with the traveller and follow up on the right items.

A workspace after the sale

The relationship continues after booking creation. Agents may need to review booking details, obtain relevant vouchers or invoices, and understand transaction information.

A digital workspace makes these routine tasks easier. It should also leave a clear path for situations that need human assistance, such as an amendment or a complex requirement.

Self-service works best when it supports the relationship your team has built with the agent.

The value comes from connecting the sale to the reservations and operational work that follows.

A useful B2B platform gives agents confidence that the product they select, the terms they accept and the service your DMC delivers belong to the same controlled process.
