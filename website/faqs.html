Understanding DMC24

Who it is for, how it works and where to start.

6 answers
What is DMC24?+

DMC24 is software for destination management companies and inbound tour operators. It connects sales and CRM, supplier contracts, quotations, bookings, reservations, ground operations, operational finance and reporting. B2B, B2C, Local Sales and Multi-DMC capabilities can extend that operational foundation.

Who is DMC24 designed for?+

It is designed for growing DMCs, established inbound operators and groups running several destination businesses. The appropriate edition depends on your destination, workflows, team size and distribution requirements—not only the size of your company.

Is DMC24 only a booking system or a Mid Office?+

Mid Office is its operational core, covering the journey from enquiry through delivery and profitability. It can also support agent distribution, direct traveller sales, destination representatives and connected destination businesses through the relevant extensions.

Can we manage both FIT and group business?+

Yes. DMC24 supports customised FIT and group/GIT workflows, including multi-service quotations, bookings, supplier coordination and operations. The exact quotation and booking capabilities should be checked against your destination’s plan matrix.

What does one connected travel file mean?+

Information captured during sales can continue into quotations, confirmed services, supplier reservations, vouchers and financial review. This gives departments a shared booking context instead of rebuilding the same journey in separate tools.

Can we start with core operations and add more capabilities later?+

Yes. The website describes a modular adoption approach: establish the operational foundation first, then add distribution channels, integrations or additional destinations. The sequence, dependencies and commercial scope are agreed during discovery.

Sales, CRM and quotations

From the first enquiry to a proposal ready for confirmation.

6 answers
How does DMC24 help the sales team manage enquiries?+

Sales users can record customer and agent enquiries, assign responsibility, organise follow-ups and review progress towards quotation and booking. CRM reporting helps managers see activity and conversion across relevant employees and markets.

Can we build tailor-made FIT and group quotations?+

Yes, where the quotation builder is included. Teams can combine contracted accommodation, transport, transfers, activities and other destination services into a costed programme, with applicable selling rules and markups.

Can we revise a quotation and retain earlier versions?+

Quote history and version management are listed in relevant Professional plans. This supports reviewing proposal changes before the agreed itinerary becomes a booking. Confirm the versioning workflow for your chosen edition during the demo.

Can we use different rates and markups for different markets?+

The platform supports configured source-market rates and commercial rules. Contracting and distribution capabilities can distinguish rates, terms and markups by market, partner or service, depending on the module and configuration.

Which itinerary and proposal formats are available?+

Relevant editions offer email, PDF and digital itineraries, with multilingual output where included. Formats vary by destination: the Maldives material, for example, focuses on email and WhatsApp quotations. Check the destination matrix rather than assuming every output format is universal.

Can an accepted quotation become a booking without rebuilding it?+

The connected workflow is designed to carry confirmed quotation information into a live travel file. Reservations and operations can then work with the agreed services, while finance tracks the related income and costs.

Products, contracts and offers

Turn your own destination knowledge into reusable rates and products.

6 answers
Can we load our own negotiated supplier contracts?+

Yes. Own contracting is a central part of DMC24. Supported products include hotels, transfers, transport, activities, entrance tickets, meals, guides and other configured destination services. Contracting availability and depth vary by edition.

Can contracts differ by season and source market?+

Yes. Relevant plans include contracting for different markets and seasons. Supplier rates and conditions need to be prepared and configured so the sales team can apply the correct commercial basis to each enquiry.

Can DMC24 manage hotel and resort offers?+

Destination-specific capabilities include single and combined offers, tactical offers and cross-season offers where supported. Rules and eligible combinations must be configured from the actual supplier contract; inclusion depends on the destination and plan.

Can we manage blackout dates, stop sales and allocations?+

These capabilities are explicitly listed in relevant destination plans, including Mauritius: blackout offer management, stop-sale and free-sale management, and allotment/allocation management. They should be demonstrated using your supplier rules before the scope is finalised.

Can we manage nationality-based fees and multi-nationality quotes?+

Configured entrance-fee rules can support nationality-based pricing. Safari destination plans also list a Multi-Nationality Quote Builder. Availability depends on the destination edition and selected variant.

Can our own contracts be used alongside third-party inventory?+

Yes. The B2B and B2C models describe a mix of your own contracted products and selected external inventory. Each supplier connection needs agreed API access, mapping, commercial terms and testing before it becomes part of the sales workflow.

Bookings, reservations and operations

Coordinate the services behind every confirmed journey.

7 answers
What booking methods does DMC24 support?+

The website describes FIT bookings, group/GIT bookings, Quick Booking and individual service booking. These serve different situations, from a complete itinerary to a standalone transfer or activity. Check which methods are included in the destination plan you select.

What is the difference between a customer booking and a supplier reservation?+

A customer booking records the services sold and the commercial arrangement with the customer or agent. Supplier reservations deal with requesting and confirming those services with hotels and other providers. DMC24 connects both sides of the travel file.

Can we issue guest vouchers and supplier documents?+

Yes. Relevant booking and reservation workflows include guest vouchers and supplier-facing documentation. The documents and formats used should match the confirmed services and the implementation’s configured templates.

How are amendments and cancellations handled?+

Booking amendments and cancellations are part of the platform’s described workflows. The effect on services, charges, supplier commitments and refunds depends on the product, channel and applicable terms, so these scenarios should form part of implementation testing.

Can operations manage vehicles, drivers, guides and movements?+

DMC24 includes transportation and logistics capabilities for guest movements, vehicle requirements, driver and guide coordination, duty slips and operational lists where included. Teams can use booking information to organise delivery and monitor outstanding actions.

Can we manage shared transfers and shared tours?+

The UAE, Oman and Saudi Arabia plan structure includes SIC Transfer and SIC Tours Management, plus clubbing of SIC services for safaris and parks. The operational rules for combining services should be configured and validated for your business.

Can we record cash handed to the field team and trip expenses?+

Safari destination plans list Imprest Management with Cash Handover. Other destination structures may use pre-tour and post-tour expense management. These are destination-specific workflows, so the relevant process and edition should be confirmed during your demo.

Finance, profitability and reporting

Understand collections, supplier commitments and the value of each file.

7 answers
Which invoices and financial documents can DMC24 produce?+

The finance pages describe proforma, deposit, advance, tax and sales invoices, with credit and debit notes in relevant editions. Availability and document formats depend on your plan and configured accounting requirements.

Can we track customer receivables and supplier payables?+

Yes. Relevant finance capabilities cover customer collections, outstanding balances, supplier obligations, payments and settlement status. The aim is to keep booking-linked financial commitments visible alongside the services being delivered.

How does file-wise profitability work?+

File-wise P&L compares booking income with recorded costs so the team can review the financial outcome of a travel file. Provisional and final views are listed in relevant plans. The usefulness of the result depends on complete and timely entry of costs, invoices and adjustments.

Can management review monthly gross profit and market performance?+

Yes. The website describes monthly GP, financial MIS, market analysis, booking reports and conversion reporting. Managers can review relevant teams, agents, source markets and destinations according to the deployed modules and access rights.

Does DMC24 support different currencies and local tax settings?+

It supports configured multi-currency workflows and destination-specific tax settings. Selected destination plans also describe VAT-related supplier classifications or withholding-tax features. Your finance team should validate the required calculations, treatment and outputs during implementation.

Does DMC24 replace our existing accounting system?+

DMC24 provides operational finance linked to travel files and can be scoped to exchange information with supported accounting systems. Whether it replaces or complements a particular accounting workflow depends on your finance requirements and the agreed integration scope.

Are dashboards and reports automatically reliable from day one?+

Reports reflect the transactions and information captured in the system. Accurate masters, consistent daily use, complete cost recording and agreed reporting definitions are important. Role-based dashboards support review, but do not remove the need for data discipline.

B2B agent booking platform

5 answers
What can agents search and book on the B2B platform?+

Depending on the agreed inventory and configuration, agents can access hotels, transfers, transport, activities, attraction tickets, day tours and packages. Supply may come from your own contracts and selected connected providers.

Can agents register online, and do we control approval?+

Yes. The B2B page describes online registration, submission of company details and documentation, KYC review, approval or rejection, commercial setup and activation. Your team controls which partners receive access.

Can each agent have different markups and credit terms?+

The B2B model supports configured agent-specific commercial rules, fixed or percentage markups, service-based pricing and approved credit facilities. Limits and terms are set according to your partner relationships and operating policies.

Are all B2B bookings instantly confirmed?+

No. The website describes both instant booking and request-for-confirmation workflows. Which one applies depends on the product, inventory source and configuration. Agents should see the appropriate status and confirmation information for the service they select.

How do B2B sales connect to our operations team?+

Confirmed sales can flow into DMC24’s booking and fulfilment workflow for reservations, supplier coordination, vouchers and operational delivery. The portal is designed as a distribution extension of your DMC rather than a disconnected sales channel.

Can the B2C website use our own brand?+

Yes. The B2C offering is described as a branded destination platform shaped around your identity, positioning, product categories and customer segments. The visual and functional scope is agreed for your implementation.

Can travellers both book online and request tailor-made trips?+

Yes. The B2C model supports configured bookings and direct enquiries, including requirements for customised travel. Instant confirmation is not assumed for every product; enquiry-based or request-based journeys can be used where appropriate.

Can travellers access vouchers, invoices and booking history?+

The B2C page describes customer registration and login, relevant booking status, confirmations, available vouchers and invoices, and past booking information. The exact account features depend on configuration.

Can the B2C platform accept online payments and serve international customers?+

Local Sales and representatives

Manage sales made after the traveller reaches the destination.

4 answers
What is DMC24 Local Sales?+

Local Sales is a destination point-of-sale model for authorised representatives, hotel desks, counters and local sales teams. It supports selling additional services while keeping booking, collection and fulfilment information connected to the wider DMC operation.

Can representatives sell several services in one transaction?+

Yes. The Local Sales page describes a cart combining services such as excursions, attraction tickets and transfers. The sale can be a new local booking or be linked to an existing passenger or travel file, depending on the workflow.

Can we track cash, card and different collection currencies?+

The Local Sales model includes configured cash, card, payment-gateway and multi-currency collection workflows. Permitted methods and currencies are controlled by the DMC and should be matched to the relevant sales location and process.

Can we manage representative commissions, refunds and performance?+

Yes. The described capabilities include commissionable sales, commission values, payouts and outstanding commissions, alongside cancellation and refund workflows. Reporting can help compare sales by representative, product, location and period.

Multi-DMC and expansion

Connect destination businesses while retaining local execution.

4 answers
What is the Multi-DMC hub-and-spoke model?+

Each destination operates its own DMC24 Mid Office, while a shared hub connects selected products and sales channels. A common B2B platform, central sales desk or API distribution layer can bring the network together without removing destination-level fulfilment responsibility.

Can each destination retain its contracts and financial processes?+

Yes. The model is designed for local supplier contracts, commercial rules, currencies, taxes, bookings, operations and finance. The implementation defines which information remains local and which is shared with central sales or management.

Can a central sales team sell several destinations?+

The Multi-DMC page describes common distribution and central sales capabilities, including multi-country programmes and proposals. Product sharing, commercial rules, ownership and handover to the responsible local DMC need to be agreed as part of the operating design.

Can we add more destinations after the first rollout?+

Yes. The suggested approach is to establish a pilot, validate the operating blueprint and onboard additional destinations in waves. Each new entity still needs its own data, configuration, training and readiness checks.

Understand what changes from one country to another.

7 answers
Why does DMC24 have destination-specific editions?+

DMCs share a common operational foundation, but supplier contracts, logistics, taxes, selling patterns and reporting needs differ. Destination pages explain the relevant workflows and plan comparisons rather than assuming one feature matrix fits every business.

Where can I see the countries currently available?+

Use the Destinations page for the current alphabetical list and open a country page to review its features and plans. If your destination is not listed, share your requirements through Request a Demo so the team can assess the appropriate scope.

What is different about the UAE, Oman and Saudi Arabia plans?+

Their plan structure includes SIC transfer and tour management and clubbing of SIC services for safaris and parks. Individual service booking is listed in Professional Plus in this structure. Review the relevant country page for the complete plan comparison.

What specialist safari features are described for Kenya and related editions?+

The safari plan structure includes imprest and cash handover, language-guide selection, park-fee forecasting, U-Turn Management, direct and circuit offers, multi-nationality quotations and charter-package quotations at the relevant levels. Advanced supplier-tax and back-to-back brochure-product capabilities are listed in higher variants, with additional cost where stated.

What does DMC24 cover for Maldives and Seychelles resort business?+

Their product material focuses on resort contracts, offers, room and meal conditions, island transfers and booking-related finance. The Maldives material describes speedboat, seaplane and domestic-flight transfer services and email/WhatsApp quotations. Use the country matrix to confirm formats and features rather than applying another destination’s inclusions.

Which Mauritius-specific features should we review?+

The Mauritius plan page lists single and combined offers, tactical and cross-season offers, blackout offers, stop-sale and free-sale controls, and allotment/allocation management in Professional. Professional Plus lists Local Sales. Confirm the complete commercial and implementation scope for your operation.

Can I use the Kerala case study as proof of a live Kerala edition?+

No. The Kerala page in Case Studies is an illustrative workflow using DMC24’s core capabilities. It does not claim a named customer deployment or a separately launched Kerala destination edition. A demo can establish how the proposed workflow applies to your business.

Plans, users, setup fees and offers

Read the destination price cards in the context of your team and scope.

10 answers
What are the differences between Business, Professional, Professional Plus and Enterprise?+

Broadly, the plans progress from core operational control to deeper sales and contracting, advanced finance and multi-branch capabilities, and enterprise integrations or distribution. Exact inclusions vary by destination, and not every destination offers every variant. The country plan comparison is the reference for your enquiry.

Where can I find the current selling price?+

Open the relevant destination page and select the billing period and team size. The displayed rates, included users and active offers reflect that destination’s published settings. Enterprise subscriptions are consultation-based where the card states this.

What currency is used for the published destination plans?+

USD is the default plan currency on the website. Any alternative billing arrangement, applicable taxes or payment terms should be confirmed in your commercial proposal.

How does the Build your team slider calculate additional users?+

Each plan has its own included-user allowance. Additional-user charges apply only above that allowance. For example, if a plan includes 10 users, selecting 10 does not add user charges; selecting 11 adds one extra user at that plan’s applicable rate.

Are additional-user rates the same for monthly and yearly plans?+

They are separate settings. Monthly billing uses the additional-user rate per month; yearly billing uses the additional-user rate per year. Do not assume the yearly additional-user amount is automatically twelve times the monthly rate.

Why can a plan become unavailable for the team size I select?+

A plan can have a configured maximum-user limit. If the selected team size exceeds that limit, the website indicates that the plan does not fit that selection. Choose a suitable higher variant or discuss your requirements with the team.

How are yearly savings shown?+

The standard subscription saving compares twelve months at the monthly base price with the yearly base price. Additional-user charges and temporary discounts can affect the total for your selected team, so compare the displayed totals for the same user count.

Is the one-time setup fee included in the subscription price?+

Setup fees are shown separately from recurring subscription charges and additional-user costs. Each destination and available plan can have its own setup fee, including Enterprise. A zero setup fee is shown as no setup fee; it should not be assumed for every plan.

Can setup fees and subscriptions have different offers?+

Yes. Setup-fee discounts are independent of recurring-plan discounts. Dated offers apply only during their configured validity period; when they expire, the original price applies again. An offer on setup does not automatically reduce subscription or additional-user charges.

Are B2B, B2C, Local Sales, integrations and customisation included in every price?+

No. These may be included, optional or separately scoped depending on the destination and edition. Check the plan matrix and obtain a proposal covering extensions, supplier connections, implementation and any custom work. Inclusion on a general feature page is not confirmation that it is included in every subscription.

Implementation, training and support

Prepare your data, people and processes for daily use.

8 answers
What are the main stages of adopting DMC24?+

The adoption guide covers understanding operations, preparing data and configuration, training by department, piloting with real business, controlled go-live, and optimisation. It emphasises a connected operating process rather than treating implementation as software activation alone.

How long will our implementation take?+

Some destination plans list setup periods, but setup and full business adoption are different milestones. Actual readiness depends on data quality, workflow decisions, integrations, training participation and scope. Confirm a realistic implementation schedule for your organisation rather than treating a listed setup period as a universal go-live guarantee.

What information should we prepare before starting?+

Prepare users and responsibilities, agents and customers, suppliers, priority products and contracts, currencies, tax requirements and commercial rules. Agree which reports and workflows are essential for the initial rollout, then clean and validate the data needed to support them.

Can existing data be migrated?+

Selected plans mention customer and supplier database migration support. The exact records, file formats, historical depth, mapping and validation must be agreed. A reference to migration support does not automatically mean every historical booking or accounting transaction is included.

Who should lead implementation within our DMC?+

The adoption guide recommends a management sponsor, a dedicated implementation owner and departmental representatives or super users. They coordinate decisions, data preparation, training and pilot transactions with the DMC24 team.

What training and ongoing support are included?+

Destination plan tables list different training-session allowances, adoption-support periods and support channels. These are edition-specific. Confirm the current entitlement, working hours, response commitments and any additional training charges in your proposal.

Can the platform be configured or customised for our business?+

Yes. Configuration and scoped customisation can be considered around your processes and requirements. Capture gaps during discovery or the pilot, distinguish essential work from later enhancements, and agree the delivery and commercial terms before implementation.

What should we confirm about hosting, backups and access?+

Relevant plans describe shared or dedicated hosting, storage and backup arrangements, while selected editions include roles and access rights. Confirm your hosting region, backup and recovery terms, data access/export arrangements, security requirements and service commitments in the agreement; the website does not define one universal policy for every deployment.

APIs, integrations and AI

Connect external technology with a clearly defined scope.

7 answers
Which types of integrations can be considered?+

The website covers travel inventory APIs, hotel channel managers, payment gateways, accounting systems, output APIs and custom enterprise connections. Availability depends on the provider, access, geography, commercial agreement and workflow to be supported.

Are all providers listed on the website ready to activate immediately?+

No. Provider names are integration examples, not a promise that every connection is active for every client. Discovery must confirm credentials, supplier approval, documentation, mappings, environments and the agreed implementation scope.

Can we connect Tally, Sage, QuickBooks or another accounting platform?+

Tally, Sage, QuickBooks and SQL Accounting appear among the website’s examples. A finance integration needs agreement on the chart of accounts, transaction flow, field mapping and reconciliation. Other systems should be assessed against the same requirements.

What is an output API used for?+

An output API can expose agreed DMC24 products or information to a wider distribution or enterprise environment. In a Multi-DMC model, it can form part of the connection between destination supply and central sales. The endpoints, access and data exchanged are defined in the integration scope.

What AI capability is currently described on the website?+

The published feature pages describe AI-assisted itinerary creation where configured, with related output options in relevant editions. This is assistance for preparing content; it should not be interpreted as a promise that every DMC24 process has autonomous AI functionality.

Does an AI-generated itinerary confirm rates, availability or reservations?+

No. An itinerary draft is not a supplier confirmation. Your team still needs to check the proposed content against applicable products, contracted rates, service availability and operational feasibility, then follow the normal reservation workflow.

Do we need our own Google Maps or Gemini setup?+

Several destination plan tables explicitly list Google Maps setup by the client and Gemini itinerary API setup by the client. Confirm which accounts, keys, usage charges and configuration are needed for your selected functions before rollout.
