Move from conversation to confirmed service through a structured destination-selling workflow.

01 — IDENTIFY

Find the Guest

Use relevant arrival, passenger or booking information.

02 — DISCOVER

Find the Opportunity

Understand what the traveller needs or recommend suitable experiences.

03 — SEARCH

Search Products

Find available excursions, tickets, transfers, transport or other services.

04 — CART

Add to Cart

Combine one or multiple services into the same transaction.

05 — BOOK

Create Booking

Create a new booking or connect services to the relevant travel file.

Collect Payment

Record or process cash, card or supported payment methods.
