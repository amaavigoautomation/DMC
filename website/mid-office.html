One Travel File. One Connected Journey.

DMC24 Mid Office carries information forward through the complete lifecycle of the booking.

01 — ENQUIRY

Capture the Opportunity

Record customer or agent requirements and assign the enquiry to the right sales user.

02 — QUOTE

Cost & Build

Combine contracted products and destination services into a structured quotation.

03 — PRESENT

Create the Itinerary

Turn costing into a professional traveller-facing proposal.

04 — CONVERT

Create the Booking

Move the confirmed programme into a live operational travel file.

05 — RESERVE

Confirm Suppliers

Manage hotel and service requests, confirmations and supplier commitments.

06 — OPERATE

Deliver the Journey

Prepare vouchers, transport, logistics and operational information.

07 — FINANCE

Invoice & Account

Manage customer billing, collections, supplier payables and operational finance.

08 — ANALYSE

Measure Performance

Understand file profitability, monthly GP, market performance and MIS.
