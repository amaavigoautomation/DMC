Multi-Destination DMC Technology

Connect Your Destinations with Multi-DMC

DMC24 connects independently operated destination companies through a shared technology and distribution hub—so your group can centralise sales, cross-sell destinations and scale without taking control away from local teams.

Hub-and-spoke architecture

Local execution. Central distribution.

Each destination runs on its own DMC24 mid office while a common hub brings the group’s products, availability and sales channels together.

01

Build locally

Each DMC manages its own suppliers, contracts, market rates, quotations, bookings, operations, finance and reporting.

02

Connect centrally

Selected inventory and products flow to a shared B2B platform, central sales desk or connected API distribution layer.

03

Sell globally

Agents and sales teams access multiple destinations through one relationship, while fulfilment returns to the responsible local DMC.

The operating model

Central where it creates leverage. Local where expertise matters.

Destination-level control

Every DMC keeps operational ownership

Independent supplier contracts, currencies, taxes and commercial rules
Local FIT and GIT quotation, reservation and operations workflows
Destination-specific vouchers, invoicing, receivables and payables
File-wise profitability, monthly gross profit and management reports
User roles and access rights aligned to each business unit
Group-level advantage

The network gains one connected commercial engine

One B2B gateway for agents to discover and book multiple destinations
Central sales teams can prepare multi-country programmes and proposals
Shared agent onboarding, credit, markup and distribution management
Group-level performance visibility across destinations and markets
A repeatable technology framework for onboarding new DMC entities

Implementation approach

Build the network in controlled stages.

Start with one destination, establish the operating blueprint and then expand across the group with lower risk and faster adoption.

PHASE 01

Discover & design

Map each destination’s processes, commercial structure, currencies, taxes, users and integration needs.

PHASE 02

Deploy the pilot

Configure one DMC, migrate essential data, train teams and validate the end-to-end operating workflow.

PHASE 03

Connect distribution

Introduce common B2B sales, output APIs, agent controls and shared commercial rules.

PHASE 04

Roll out & optimise

Onboard additional destinations in waves and strengthen group reporting, governance and performance.
