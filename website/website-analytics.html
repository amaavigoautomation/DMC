How to read this report

  • Tracking starts from this publication and only after a visitor allows analytics. No historical traffic is reconstructed.
  • Visitors are estimated by browser identifier; different devices, cleared storage and declined consent affect counts. A visit ends after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Country is approximate, based on the connection location; VPNs can change it. Unknown locations remain labelled Unknown.
  • Brochure requests count successful requests from consenting visits; they are not a count of completed file downloads. Demo clicks measure interest, not confirmed enquiries.
  • Brochure conversion = matching visits with a brochure request ÷ matching visits. Filters apply to events; metrics are not whole-site totals when a page or product is selected.
  • Owner activity, private administration pages, common bots and browsers sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are excluded.
  • No names, emails or raw IP addresses are stored here. Browsing histories are not linked to named brochure enquiries. Reporting supports date ranges up to 180 days.
